Frequently Asked Questions

How does Assan Loan differ from Paisabazaar or BankBazaar?

While traditional aggregators only offer loan comparisons, Assan Loan uniquely integrates direct verified owner properties across Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Bengaluru, and other top metros with zero brokerage. You can select an owner's flat and immediately secure an 8.35% linked home loan in one seamless journey.

What documents are required for instant loan processing?

For salaried applicants: PAN Card, Aadhaar, last 3 months salary slips, 6 months bank statement, and Form 16. For self-employed: PAN Card, Aadhaar, 2 years ITR with computation, and 12 months bank statements.

Can I transfer my existing home loan to reduce interest rate?

Yes! With Home Loan Balance Transfer, you can switch your high-interest loan (e.g. 9.5%+) to a top bank at 8.35%, saving ₹5 Lakhs to ₹15 Lakhs over your remaining tenure.
