Privacy Policy

Rainbow AI by LeadsIndia · Effective: 10 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LeadsIndia and its Rainbow AI services collect, use, store and protect information when you use our website, AI tools, lead-management features and authorized integrations.

Information we collect

Depending on the feature you use, we may process account and contact information, business and property information, leads and campaign-related data, technical logs, device/browser information, and information you explicitly authorize through third-party services such as Meta.

How we use information

We use information to provide and secure our services, manage leads and workflows, generate AI-assisted plans, support authorized advertising integrations, troubleshoot errors, prevent abuse, comply with law, and improve service reliability.

Meta platform data

When a user or business authorizes a Meta integration, Rainbow AI processes only the data and permissions required for the enabled feature. Access credentials are intended to be stored server-side and are not intentionally exposed in public pages. We do not claim to publish campaigns, spend advertising funds, or send bulk communications unless the relevant action is actually authorized and completed.

AI processing

Some user-provided instructions and business context may be processed by AI service providers to generate requested outputs. Users should not submit passwords, payment-card details or other secrets in ordinary prompts.

Sharing and processors

We may use hosting, infrastructure, analytics, communications, AI and platform providers as necessary to operate the service. We do not sell personal information as a standalone data-broker product. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to protect users and the service.

Security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including access controls and server-side handling of integration credentials. Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant service, legal, security and operational purposes.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of personal information associated with our service, and you may revoke third-party permissions through the relevant provider.

Data deletion

Instructions for requesting deletion are available on our Data Deletion page.

International users

Our services may be accessed from multiple countries. Where applicable, we process information in accordance with relevant privacy and data-protection obligations and the terms governing connected platforms.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service, integrations or legal requirements evolve. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact
